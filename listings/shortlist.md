## Quick reads

- **Best €/m² (rooms):** Lijsterbeslaan (€40) — biggest room for the money.
- **Best €/m² overall:** the whole flats Vlamingstraat & Mercuriusweg (€26), then Kloksteeg (€34) — far more space per euro than the rooms.
- **Closest to campus:** Oude Delft (~5 min) and Herman Gorterhof (~5–10 min).
- **Cheapest:** Prof. Telderslaan (€500) — but smallest (10 m², €50/m²) and no living room.
- **Note:** biking times are approximate; verify with a maps route for your exact daily destination on campus.
